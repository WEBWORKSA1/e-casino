The formula

P(at least one success in N tries) = 1 − (1 − p)^N Tries needed for target T: N = ln(1 − T) ÷ ln(1 − p)

Notice what happens when N equals the “1 in X” figure: the answer is always close to 63%, because (1 − 1/N)^N approaches 1/e ≈ 36.8% as N grows.

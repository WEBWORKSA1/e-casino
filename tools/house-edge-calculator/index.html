Each game uses its own typical pace, so fast games (slots) cost more per hour even at similar edges.

The formula casinos use

Expected loss (“theo”) = average bet × bets per hour × hours × house edge Example: $10 × 70 hands × 3 hours × 0.5% = $10.50 for a session of blackjack with perfect basic strategy.

Casinos use exactly this number to rate players and decide comps, which is why a free buffet is never free. Your actual result in any one session will swing above or below the expectation — see the Bankroll & Risk Calculator for the range — but over many sessions, results converge on it. More in House Edge Explained and How Casinos Make Money.

Tip: treat the per-session number as a ticket price. If a three-hour night out costs an expected $10 at blackjack or $120 at the slots, you can decide what that entertainment is worth to you. See How to Set an Entertainment Budget.

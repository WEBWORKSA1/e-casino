Why “e”?

The casino's favorite constant

Take any 1-in-N shot and try it N times. You'll miss every single time about 36.8% of the time — that's 1/e, where e ≈ 2.71828. Bet a roulette number for 37 spins and there's a 36.3% chance it never hits. Our name is a reminder that intuition about luck is usually wrong, and math is usually right.

The conversions

Implied probability = 1 ÷ decimal odds Decimal = fractional + 1 (3/2 → 2.50) American +X → decimal = 1 + X/100 American −X → decimal = 1 + 100/X Overround = Σ implied probabilities − 100%

A standard −110 / −110 line implies 52.38% for each side — 104.76% in total. That extra 4.76% is the overround; it is why a bettor needs to win 52.38% of such bets just to break even. Full explanation in Odds Formats Explained and Sports Betting Math.

Reality check: implied probability is the price, not the truth. A bet only has positive expected value if your honest estimate of the probability exceeds the implied figure — and the margin makes that rare.

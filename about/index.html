Why “e”?

Partly because e stands for edge — the house edge is the center of everything we teach — and for education. But mostly because of Euler's number, e ≈ 2.71828. Take any one-in-N chance and try it N times, and you'll miss every single time about 1/e ≈ 36.8% of the time. Bet one roulette number for 37 spins and there's roughly a 36% chance it never comes up. Intuition says “it's bound to hit eventually”; e says otherwise. That gap between intuition and math is exactly what we exist to close.

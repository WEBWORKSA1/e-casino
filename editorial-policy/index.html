Editorial policy

How we decide what's true, how we keep money away from the math, and what happens when we get something wrong.

1. Independence

e.casino accepts no payment, commission or free play from casinos, sportsbooks, game studios or affiliate programs. We do not rank, review or link to gambling operators. This removes the conflict of interest that shapes much of the gambling web.

2. How we verify numbers

  • House edges and probabilities are derived from first principles (exact counting, combinatorics, expected value) or taken from standard, widely published game math, and stated with the rules they assume.
  • Key figures are cross-checked with our own Monte Carlo simulations, which you can run yourself in our labs.
  • Hypothetical examples are labeled as hypothetical. We don't invent statistics, studies, quotes or testimonials.
  • Where rules vary between venues, we say so and show how the numbers change.

3. Corrections

If you find an error, tell us. We review every report, fix confirmed errors promptly, and update the page's “updated” date. Substantive corrections are noted on the page.

4. Advertising & sponsorship

We display advertising served by Google AdSense, which selects ads automatically; we do not choose individual advertisers and we opt out of gambling ad categories where available. Sponsorships are labeled, never influence editorial content, and may not promote real-money gambling on editorial pages. Reader contributions never buy coverage.

5. Responsible gaming

We never encourage anyone to gamble. Our content is intended for adults and treats gambling as paid entertainment with a predictable cost. Relevant pages link to free, confidential help.

6. Tools & automation

We use software, including simulations and automated checks, to help produce and verify content. Every published guide is reviewed by our editorial team before publication. Automated headline feeds on our news page link to their original publishers and are labeled as such.

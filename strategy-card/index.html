Numbers worth remembering

  • Expected loss = bet × bets per hour × hours × house edge
  • Blackjack paying 6:5 instead of 3:2 adds ≈1.4% to the edge
  • Insurance carries an edge of roughly 7% — skip it
  • Two dice: 7 comes up 1 time in 6
  • Pocket pair in Hold'em: 1 in 17 · pocket aces: 1 in 221
  • A 1-in-N shot tried N times misses entirely ≈37% of the time
  • Past results never change the next independent outcome

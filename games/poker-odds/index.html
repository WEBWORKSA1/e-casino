How to read equity

Equity is your share of the pot if the hand were played out from this point thousands of times: wins count fully, ties count as a split. Against five random opponents, pocket aces win roughly half the time — a huge edge over the 1-in-6 fair share, but far from certain.

Equity = P(win) + P(tie) ÷ (number of players sharing the pot) Pot odds check: call if equity > call ÷ (pot + call)

Estimates come from random simulation, so they vary by a fraction of a percent between runs; more deals means more precision. Learn the shortcuts in Texas Hold'em Odds: Outs, Pot Odds & the Rule of 2 and 4 and the full table in Poker Hand Rankings & Probabilities.

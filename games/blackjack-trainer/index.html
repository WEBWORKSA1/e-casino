How to use the trainer

Each round deals you two cards and shows one dealer card, exactly as at a real table. Choose Hit, Stand, Double or Split. The trainer grades your choice against the mathematically optimal play for these rules and explains the reasoning in one or two lines. Correct answers advance automatically so you can build speed; mistakes pause so the lesson sticks.

Use Hard, Soft or Pairs mode to drill the categories your weak-spot map flags. When you are consistently above 95%, switch on Hide and play from memory. The 50-hand Challenge produces a score code you can submit to the monthly Strategy Sprint.

Why basic strategy matters

Basic strategy is the set of decisions that maximizes your expected value for every possible starting hand. It does not make blackjack a winning game, but with good rules it shrinks the house edge to roughly 0.5% — among the lowest in the casino. Guessing players routinely give back several times that. Read the full chart explained in Blackjack Basic Strategy, then see the long-run cost of any game in the House Edge Calculator.

Reality check: perfect strategy still loses slowly over time. The trainer is for understanding the game and making smarter decisions, not a path to profit.

Frequently asked questions

What rules does the trainer use?

Four to eight decks, dealer stands on soft 17, double on any two cards, double after split allowed, no surrender. These are common multi-deck rules and the chart matches them exactly.

How much does perfect basic strategy reduce the house edge?

With good multi-deck rules, perfect basic strategy cuts the house edge to roughly 0.5%. Typical players who guess make mistakes that can cost several times that.

Is this a real-money game?

No. The trainer has no money, no chips of value and no prizes for outcomes. It only grades your decisions.

Why 7 rules the table

There are six ways to make 7 (1-6, 2-5, 3-4, 4-3, 5-2, 6-1) and only one way to make 2 or 12. That single asymmetry decides every payout on a craps layout. The Pass Line wins 244 of every 495 games on average — 49.29% — which is where its 1.41% edge comes from.

P(Pass wins) = 8/36 + Σ over points [P(point) × P(point before 7)] = 244/495 ≈ 49.29% House edge = P(lose) − P(win) = 251/495 − 244/495 = 7/495 ≈ 1.41%

Roll 10,000 times and the bars hug the gold line. Roll 30 times and they don't — and that short-run noise is what makes people believe in hot and cold dice. See also the Coin-Flip Streak Lab and The Law of Large Numbers.

# e.casino — Idea selection & revenue model

## The decision

**Build: "e.casino — Casino Math Academy."** An education-first site that explains casino games with honest math. It offers free interactive labs (no money, no prizes) and responsible-gaming resources. Monetization layers are AdSense, sponsorships, B2B domain/partnership leads, donations and skill contests.

### Why not the obvious idea (a casino review / affiliate site)?

| Model | Revenue per 1,000 visitors* | Compatible with AdSense? | Legal/ops burden | Verdict |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Casino affiliate (reviews, bonuses, CPA/rev-share) | Highest: commonly quoted CPAs run in the hundreds of dollars per depositing player | **No.** Google classes affiliate/aggregator gambling sites as "restricted", which means fewer ad buyers or none | High: licensing/registration in many regulated markets, geo-blocking, age-gating, constant compliance | Wrong fit for an AdSense mandate |
| Free "social casino" games site | Low–medium | Allowed, but thin game portals rarely pass AdSense review and attract minors | Medium | Weak |
| **Education + labs + lead gen (chosen)** | AdSense RPM plus sponsorships, leads and donations | **Yes.** Google's policy names educational content as eligible for unrestricted ads | Low | **Best risk-adjusted path** |

*Assumptions, not guarantees. Verify current numbers before acting.*

**The hard truth:** AdSense will never be this domain's biggest payday. The single largest value event is a **domain sale or lease** to an operator or brand. The site's job is to:
1. earn steady AdSense and sponsorship revenue,
2. build traffic, backlinks and brand signals that raise the domain's value, and
3. generate qualified inbound offers through the Partner page and the top-bar contact link on every page.

## Revenue model (12-month scenario, explicit assumptions)

| Driver | Assumption | Monthly at 50k visits |
|---|---|---|
| AdSense | 2.2 pages/visit; RPM $4–$10 for education traffic weighted to Tier-1 countries | $440–$1,100 |
| Lab/contest sponsors | 1–2 sponsors at $250–$500 (Partner page pricing) | $250–$1,000 |
| Donations | 0.05% of visitors × $20 average | ~$500 |
| B2B leads | Domain sale or lease, section partnership | Lumpy; one deal can exceed several years of ads |

**Traffic ramp (typical for new domains, not promised):** months 1–3: little search traffic, focus on indexing and backlinks. Months 4–9: 5k–30k visits/month if guides rank and embeds earn links. Months 10–24: 30k–150k with 100+ articles and steady link building.

## Growth levers built in

1. **Embeddable calculators** (`/embed/`): every embed carries a credit backlink, which compounds authority. No competitor we reviewed offers this.
2. **Graded trainers and simulators:** long session times mean more ad impressions per visit. They are also shareable and good for "free blackjack trainer" style searches.
3. **Daily puzzle and self-updating news** (workflow): fresh signals and repeat visits.
4. **Learning paths and the 7-day Academy:** capture emails, which are your owned audience and the base for sponsor deals.
5. **Monthly skill contests:** free entry, skill-only, sponsor-funded prizes. They bring in user-generated activity and give sponsors inventory to buy.

## Risks & mitigations

- **AdSense review rejection** ("low value content"). Mitigation: 31 long-form originals, 10 tools, glossary and complete legal pages are already in place. Publish 2–3 new guides weekly before and after applying. Don't add casino links.
- **Domain/brand confusion with "eCasino" marks.** Mitigation: the brand is always written as the web address "e.casino", with a non-affiliation disclosure on every page and a full trademark page.
- **Gambling-adjacent ad sensitivity.** Mitigation: block gambling ad categories in AdSense. Keep sponsorships free of real-money promotion on editorial pages.
- **Helpline data going stale.** Mitigation: helplines live on one page with a "checked" date. Re-verify every quarter.
